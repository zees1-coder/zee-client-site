Strictly fair play

Zee Client is legit-only by design. No ESP, no combat cheats, no chunk finders — and that's a hard boundary, not a settings toggle. Play on your favourite servers without worrying about what's under the hood.

The launcher

Not just a mod. A whole command centre.

Zee Launcher installs Minecraft and Fabric for you — then keeps going. Instances, a full Modrinth browser, live server pings, a game library, PC tools and a customisable dashboard, all in one clean glass UI.

▣

Instances & mods

Spin up instances on any version, browse the entire Modrinth catalogue, and auto-install dependencies in one click.

⚑

Servers & logs

Real server-list pings with live player counts and latency, plus a log and crash viewer that points at the culprit mod.

♠

Game library

Import your Steam and Epic games, track playtime and ratings, and launch anything on your PC from one place.

◰

Workspaces

One click opens your apps, sites, power plan and scene — a Gaming layout, a Coding layout, whatever you set up.

⚡

Performance mode

Frees up your PC before you play: switches power plan and closes the background apps you choose, then restores it all.

◉

Dashboard & scenes

A customisable widget dashboard, twelve animated backgrounds, weather, a music player and more — make it yours.
