Modules

Everything useful. Nothing shady.

Every module is toggleable, rebindable, and rendered through Zee's custom animated GUI system.

Fullbright

🎥

Freecam

Detach your camera to scout builds and plan projects from any angle.

🔍

Zoom

Smooth, scroll-adjustable zoom on a keybind — spot things from far across the map.

⌨️

Keystrokes

An on-screen WASD overlay that lights up with your inputs. Great for PvP clips.

📋

ArrayList HUD

A clean list of your active modules, always visible in the corner of your screen.

🎛️

Full keybind system

Bind any module to any key, straight from the animated in-game GUI.

🛡️

Strictly fair play

Zee Client is legit-only by design. No ESP, no combat cheats, no chunk finders — and that's a hard boundary, not a settings toggle. Play on your favourite servers without worrying about what's under the hood.
